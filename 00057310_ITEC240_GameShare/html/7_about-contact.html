GAME SHARE ORIGIN

Meet Joe Brown, a father of three, who created this nonprofit website to give children aged 7 to 14 a fun and inspiring space to play games made by their peers, and hopefully spark their own interest in game development.

OUR INSPIRATION

Game Share was inspired by our three children: Juli Brown, Ken Brown, and Ryu Brown who loved creating and playing their own games in Scratch. However, they often struggled to share their creations with friends. That challenge sparked the idea for Game Share, a platform designed to make it easy for our children and kids around the world to create and share their games effortlessly.

WHO WE ARE

We're everyday people, just like you, who love creating and sharing meaningful content, whether it's a video game, a website, or a piece of art. We believe everyone has a unique talent to offer, and we encourage you to share yours with the world too!
